SHEGURU — FIRST LAPTOP APPLICATION

1. Extract this ZIP completely into Documents\SHEguru.
2. Install Python 3.10 or newer if it is not already installed:
   https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
3. Double-click Start-SHEguru.bat.
4. Keep the black command window open. Your browser should open SHEguru.
5. Start with Settings, or choose Explore with demo records on Overview.

Do not double-click web/index.html. It needs the local app server.

The app saves your records in data\sheguru.sqlite3.
Download a full backup from Settings regularly.

This is the first evaluation version. Payroll integration, online training,
certificate issuance, incident management and risk registers come in later stages.
See README.md for details and backup/restore instructions.

VERSION 0.3: APPOINTMENT AND DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS
Appointments: review the listed requirements, assign people and link signed letters.
Documents: review company document requirements and upload the evidence.
Reports: see missing items and print / save a PDF report.
Not every starter item applies to every company. Record applicability decisions.
Before updating an existing installation, download a full backup and keep the old
folder. See README.md for safe update and restore steps.
