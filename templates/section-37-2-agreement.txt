2. AGREED ARRANGEMENTS AND PROCEDURES
The parties record below the specific arrangements and procedures intended to
support compliance by the contractor in carrying out the defined work. Complete
each allocation for the actual job; a signature alone does not implement controls.

RESPONSIBILITY SCHEDULE

Address, where applicable:
- Workplace hazards, site information, risk assessments and safe work methods.
- Coordination, inductions, training, competence and supervision.
- Written appointments, permits, access restrictions and required authorisations.
- Equipment suitability, inspections, maintenance, isolation and defect control.
- PPE selection, provision, use and relevant health / exposure controls.
- Emergency arrangements, first aid, evacuation and communication.
- Incident / near-miss reporting, investigation and statutory notifications.
- Subcontractor arrangements, worker consultation and interface risks.
- Required registers, records and retention / access arrangements.
- Change control, corrective actions, monitoring and escalation of unsafe work.
